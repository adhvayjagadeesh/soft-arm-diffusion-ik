Summary.txt - Supplementary video contents
===========================================

The video (32.5 s) accompanies the manuscript "When Does Multimodal
Diffusion Inverse Kinematics Help Soft Continuum Arms - and Does It
Survive Model Mismatch?" and contains two scenes rendered directly
from the paper's trained models and evaluation protocol:

Scene 1 (Conditional diffusion sampling): eight candidate arm
configurations evolve from noise into distinct inverse-kinematics
solutions for the same tip target, visualizing the DDIM denoising
trajectory described in Sec. III of the paper.

Scene 2 (Obstacle avoidance, two trials): for a tip target with three
randomly placed spherical obstacles (the paper's E3 task), the
deterministic MLP baseline's single answer (red, dashed) fails, while
the diffusion model's candidate set includes accurate,
collision-free solutions (blue); orange candidates are accurate but
colliding, gray candidates inaccurate.

The paper is fully self-contained; the video is illustrative only.
